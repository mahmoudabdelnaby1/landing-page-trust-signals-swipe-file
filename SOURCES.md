# Regulatory Compliance & Consumer Protection Standards

Trust signals and disclosure badges in this repository are aligned with statutory consumer protection and e-commerce laws in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Egypt.

### Official Statutory References
1. **Saudi Ministry of Commerce - E-Commerce Executive Regulations:**
   - Official Portal: https://mc.gov.sa/
   - Requirements for displaying Commercial Registration (CR), merchant contact data, and return/refund policies.
2. **Saudi General Authority for Media Regulation (GAMR):**
   - Official Portal: https://gcam.gov.sa/
   - Mawthooq platform advertising licensing standards and mandatory license number placement.
3. **Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA):**
   - E-Invoicing & VAT Display: https://zatca.gov.sa/
   - Mandatory VAT registration number display and gross price presentation rules (15% in KSA).
4. **Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency (CPA):**
   - Official Regulations: http://www.cpa.gov.eg/
   - Right of return within 14 days and clear price disclosure in Egyptian Pounds.
